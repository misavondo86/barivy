Six pages, one pill

Everything sits behind a single hover. No dock clutter, no extra windows — the notch expands, you do the thing, it folds away.

Music that shows its lyrics

Barivy reads whatever is already playing on Windows — Spotify, a browser tab, your media player — and follows it with synced lyrics fetched from LRCLIB. The artwork tints the notch with a soft aurora that moves with the music.

  • Synced lyrics in the notch, line by line, with the current line sharp and the rest gently blurred
  • Fullscreen lyrics view for a full lean-back moment (Pro)
  • Volume for the player itself, separate from system volume (Pro), with the system slider as fallback
  • The pill keeps singing — a mini lyric line and live weather share the collapsed pill

Deadlines that count down out loud

A todo list is easy to ignore. Barivy deadlines pick an exact day and time — chosen with a picker, never typed — and keep a live countdown on every card: 2d 3h left, 45m left, updating every minute.

  • Warned three times: a day ahead, five minutes ahead, and exactly on time — with a Windows alert sound (Pro)
  • Startup reminder each morning for your nearest deadline (Pro)
  • Free keeps the core: add, done, delete, clear done — plus date-only deadlines with a due-day reminder

A shelf for the files you keep reaching for

Pin the poster draft, the mix bounce, the catalog PDF — drag files onto the Shelf and they wait in the notch instead of a buried folder. Click an image or a video and it previews right there.

  • Drag in, drag out — tiles drag back into Explorer or your design app
  • Built-in preview for images, with video and audio playback in a floating player (video & audio: Pro)
  • Honest about missing files: moved a file? Its tile greys out, with a one-click clear

Color tools built for designers

Press the picker and Barivy reads the exact pixel under your cursor, anywhere on screen — another app, a photo, a client PDF. The HEX copies itself. Then turn any color into a five-swatch palette, or pull the five dominant colors out of an image.

  • Fullscreen picker with a recent-colors history (6 free · 12 Pro)
  • Palette generator: Mono, Analogous, or Complementary — click any swatch to copy
  • Image Palette reads PNG, JPG, BMP, and GIF files

Notes, in place

A scratch page inside the notch for set lists, scripts, and the thought you would otherwise lose.

Weather in the pill

Live conditions for your area refresh every 15 minutes via Open-Meteo — and politely hide while a lyric line is showing.

Pin & game mode

Pin the notch open while you work, or flip on game mode and hover-expansion stays out of your fullscreen game entirely.

Make it yours

Accent themes led by Tan Gold, Solid or Aurora backgrounds, start-with-Windows from the tray. Custom colors and translucent aurora are Pro.

Small, honest, and offline by default

No account, no Barivy server, no telemetry. Your todos, shelf paths, colors, and notes are stored as plain files on your own machine.

The Windows warning is expected

Barivy is an independent app without a paid code-signing certificate, so the first time you download and run the installer, your browser and Windows SmartScreen may show warnings. Those messages appear for almost every small indie app — they mean “new and unsigned”, not “dangerous”. Getting past them takes three short steps:

STEP 1

Keep the download

If your browser says BarivySetup.exe is not commonly downloaded, choose Keep, then Keep anyway, so the installer finishes downloading.

STEP 2

More info, then Run anyway

Run the installer. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway.

STEP 3

Follow the installer

Click through the setup wizard. Barivy installs per user — no admin rights needed — adds a Start Menu shortcut, and settles into the top of your screen.

Privacy, plainly

Barivy keeps everything on your PC, in %LocalAppData%\Barivy. It makes exactly three kinds of network call, all visible and all replaceable by doing nothing:

Weather

Current conditions from Open-Meteo, refreshed every 15 minutes, shown in the collapsed pill. No account and no key involved.

Lyrics

Synced lyrics are fetched from LRCLIB by track title and artist — the same information any lyrics lookup needs.

Update check

Barivy reads one small text file — the latest version number, a download link, and the changelog. Free checks only when you click it in the tray; Pro can check once a day.

Your license key

Validated offline with public-key cryptography on your own machine. It is never transmitted, and Barivy works fully offline once unlocked.

FAQ

What people ask before downloading.

What does Barivy need to run?

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, and the .NET 10 Desktop Runtime (a free Microsoft download, installed once). Barivy itself installs from BarivySetup.exe — just download it and run it.

Is the free version limited in time?

No. Free is free forever, with the full notch, music, todo, shelf, and color tools. Pro only deepens specific features — the split is in the table above.

Which music players does it follow?

Anything Windows itself can see as a media session — Spotify, browsers, and most desktop players. Lyrics come from LRCLIB when a synced match exists for the track.

Will it get in the way of games?

Turn on game mode from the tray and hover-expansion pauses while a fullscreen app or game has focus. The tray menu and pin lock still work as usual.

How do updates work?

Download the new BarivySetup.exe and run it over your existing install. Your settings, todos, and license live in %LocalAppData%\Barivy, untouched by the update.

How do I uninstall it?

Uninstall Barivy from Windows Settings, under Apps — that removes the app and its Start Menu shortcut. Your data in %LocalAppData%\Barivy is kept, so a reinstall picks up right where you left off; delete that folder yourself only if you want every trace gone.
